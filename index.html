FAQ

How do I play?

Swipe to slide every tile. Two of the same creature merge and evolve into the next stage. Reach the 2048 tile — a human — to win, then keep evolving.

Can I use my own pictures?

Yes. Open the menu, tap Create your own theme, and give any stage an emoji or a photo. Photos never leave your device.

I bought Pro on a new phone. How do I get it back?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, open the menu and tap Restore purchases.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Your games, themes and badges are saved only on your device, so deleting the app removes them.

Does the game need the internet?

No. Everything works offline. Ads and purchases need a connection.
