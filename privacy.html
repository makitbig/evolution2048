Privacy Policy

Evolution 2048 · Effective November 1, 2026

The short version

  • We have no servers and no accounts. Your games, themes and photos stay on your device.
  • Ads in the free version are served by Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers.
  • We use Google Firebase to count anonymous gameplay events and to receive crash reports.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

1. Data stored on your device

Your games in progress, scores, badges, settings and the themes you create — including any photos and emoji you add — are stored only in the app's storage on your device. We cannot see them, and they are deleted when you delete the app. Photos you pick are cropped and resized on your device and are never uploaded.

2. Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes. This includes events such as starting a game, reaching a stage, using an item or opening the Pro screen, together with device information (model, operating system, app version, country and an app-instance identifier). It does not include your photos, theme names or anything you type. See Firebase privacy information.

3. Advertising

The free version shows a small banner and optional rewarded videos from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and ad interactions to show and measure ads. On iOS you will be asked whether to allow tracking; if you decline, you will only see non-personalized ads. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. Buying Pro removes the banner. See how Google uses data for ads.

4. Purchases

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made, but never your name, payment card or billing details.

5. Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply, and delete them when the conversation is over.

6. Children

Evolution 2048 is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has contacted us, email us and we will delete the information.

7. Your choices

  • Turn off personalized ads in your device's privacy or Google settings.
  • Delete all game data by deleting the app.
  • Ask us any question about your data at the address below.

8. Changes and contact

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.

Blucos · support@blucos.com
