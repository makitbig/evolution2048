Blucos Uploader

Blucos Games' internal tool for posting our own game videos to our own YouTube channel.

What it is

Blucos is a small independent studio that makes mobile puzzle games, starting with Evolution 2048. Blucos Uploader is a small command-line script that runs on our own computer. It takes short gameplay videos that we make ourselves and uploads them, at the time we choose, to our own YouTube channel, Blucos Games (@blucosgames).

  • It is used only by Blucos. It is not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else.
  • It signs in to exactly one Google account, our channel's, using Google OAuth 2.0.
  • It only uploads videos and reads our own channel's name. It does not read, store or change anyone else's data.

Uses YouTube API Services

Blucos Uploader uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
